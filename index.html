Lembaran Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Lembaran is a document editor for Android, Windows, macOS and Linux. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

Lembaran does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your documents never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

Your documents

  • Documents you open are read only on your device.
  • Documents you save are written to your device's storage (on Android: Documents › Lembaran), or to the place you choose.
  • When you tap Share, Lembaran hands the file to the app you pick (for example email, Google Drive or WhatsApp). What happens next is governed by that app's own privacy policy.
  • When you Print on Android, Lembaran creates a temporary PDF on your device and opens it in your PDF viewer.

Permissions (Android)

PermissionWhy
Storage (Android 10 and older only)To save your documents in the Documents folder. Newer Android versions don't need this permission.
InternetIncluded by the app framework. Lembaran does not send your documents or any data over the internet.

Lembaran does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or any other personal information.

Children

Lembaran is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
